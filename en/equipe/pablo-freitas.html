His Background

Artistic creation is a field in which I strive to explore all facets, seeking not only to learn but also to understand this infinite and eternally inspiring universe.

After four years of acting training at EICAR, I discovered for the first time the fascinating world of directing. From my theatrical background, I quickly wished to steer my work towards cinema. Indeed, the power of the big screen allows me to push the boundaries of imagination, both through sets and camera perspectives.

It was by meeting Jérémy Lavalade that I found the perfect opportunity to fully invest myself in this new path. Harmony of Doubt is an ambitious project that allows me not only to collaborate with passionate and committed individuals from diverse backgrounds but also to create around shared values of respect and diversity.

I am convinced that this approach will allow the film to stand out, as the tone and the ecological and societal issues addressed in Harmony of Doubt are absolutely essential today. They must be heard by everyone, and I am confident that this project will resonate with minds and awaken consciences.
