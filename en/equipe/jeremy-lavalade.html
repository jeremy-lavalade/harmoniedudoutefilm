Jérémy LAVALADE

Producer & Co-Director

His Background

All my decisions are based on three fundamental pillars:
Diversity, Tolerance and Entrepreneurship.

After graduating with honors from a Scientific Baccalaureate, I was particularly drawn to ESSEC Business School for its international and multicultural values.
I was able to further develop the skills I acquired at ESSEC by subsequently managing projects, some worth several million euros, in the Medical, Digital, and Real Estate sectors. While I stood out for my ability to handle Corporate Strategy and Management issues with rigor, these experiences primarily revealed to me the concerning lack of environmental awareness within companies.

Beyond my academic and professional journey, I’ve had the opportunity to travel extensively. I have always been in awe of the diversity and beauty of our planet. However, I have also witnessed numerous landscapes that are, day by day, being increasingly damaged by human activity.
Moreover, I have always been passionate about art, particularly architecture and cinema. Inspired by the complementary worlds of Jean Cocteau, Alfred Hitchcock, and Wes Anderson, I see my life as a true journey, blending intellectual exploration, artistic discovery, and entrepreneurial spirit.
My constant thirst for knowledge, my desire to push boundaries, and my ability to turn ideas into concrete actions have now led me to a new path: the production of the feature film Harmony of Doubt.

Thus, while my goal is to contribute to the transformation of our society, I simply seek to bring meaning to a world that doesn’t always seem to have it.

Jérémy Lavalade
