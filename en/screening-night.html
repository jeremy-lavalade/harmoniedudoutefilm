June 19, 2026 — Cinéma Le Central, Puteaux

The Screening Night

An exclusive evening dedicated to the project: the presentation, the teaser, the Q&A with the audience and the behind-the-scenes — relive it in four videos.

01 — The presentation

“The film is only an introductory sentence”

The night opens with the birth of the project: a feature film imagined by Jérémy Lavalade from a handwritten notebook written almost forty years earlier. Between intimate origins, a presentation of the universe and reflections on doubt, truth, critical thinking and environmental issues, this recording unveils the film's intentions — up to the interviews with the production designers, the production manager, the lead actor and the composer of the original score.

The presentation of the project, from the Notebook to Harmony of Doubt

02 — The teaser

Welcome to La Pointe Rose

Then the lights go down. This short film is the first gateway into the film's universe. It does not tell everything: it leaves a trace, a sensation, a first crack. As the notebook already said — this film is only an introductory sentence.

The teaser, screened on the big screen that night

03 — The Q&A with the audience

Questions from the audience

The screening continued with an exchange with the audience about the project, its origins and its intentions: doubt, truth, trust, collective illusions and our relationship to environmental issues. Through the audience's reactions, the film reveals itself differently — as an invitation to question what we think we know.

The audience's questions — and the set photography of Juliette Cortade

04 — The making of

Behind the scenes of La Pointe Rose

To close the night, a look behind the curtain. Directed by Émile Balcaen, the making of tells the story of the teaser's creation: sets, costumes, staging, constraints — every member of this human adventure pursued the same goal, bringing a film to life in a harmonious and colorful world.

The making of the short film

Want to stay behind the scenes? The shooting of the teaser can also be relived in 166 photographs, all taken by Juliette Cortade, our set photographer.
