Despite the rise in sea level, one land is still emerged:

THE ISLAND OF

LA POINTE ROSE

Discover the Island

The symbiosis between humans, technology, and nature
in a virtuous and calming world.

What if we did this in our world...

Film Goals

Although the tragic rise in sea levels has made La Pointe Rose the last piece of land above water on the planet, a people live there peacefully, free from negative thoughts. To protect and restore their final ecosystem, the Pontérosains have united to develop environmentally friendly technologies.

Tana, in her thirties, is a journalist. Appreciated and respected by everyone, she enjoys her daily life on La Pointe Rose.

At dawn, as the city slowly wakes, Tana finds a newspaper in the street.
This seemingly innocuous event, however, sends her into a disturbing tailspin.

With a dull anxiety, she intuitively feels that the harmony reigning on the island might be just an illusion.
She must understand. She is not sure. She even doubts whether she truly wants to know…
