Tone of
Harmony of Doubt

The story of Harmony of Doubt is resolutely optimistic.

La Pointe Rose represents an ideal and inspiring world, a calm and appealing place brought to life by the story's characters. Set in an environmentally respectful future deliberately placed in the near term, this world allows viewers to easily relate to the characters and feel personally engaged in their thoughts.

The tone of the film will not be moralizing but rather cautionary: the real aim is to raise public awareness through this suspenseful film.
