The Association "L'Harmonie du Doute"

L'HARMONIE DU DOUTE is an Association governed by the french law of 1 July 1901 and the decree of 16 August 1901, registered in the French Register of Companies and Establishments (SIRENE) under the SIREN number 923 207 963 and registered with the Prefecture of Hauts-de-Seine (France) under the number RNA W922020084 since 27 February 2023.
With its registered office at 31 rue Roque de Fillol, 92800 Puteaux, France, L'HARMONIE DU DOUTE is represented by Mr. Jérémy LAVALADE, its President.

L'HARMONIE DU DOUTE is a registered association, registered under the APE code 5911A Production of films and television programmes, and has the following objectives:
- produce, direct, edit, distribute and promote cinematographic films, television programmes, multimedia videos, sound recordings and music publishing within the framework of any artistic, audiovisual and cinematographic project; design, manufacture and use of costumes as part of any artistic, audiovisual and cinematographic project;
- direction of actors and extras in the context of any artistic, audiovisual and cinematographic project;
- make-up of actors and extras as part of any audiovisual and cinematographic artistic project;
- promotion, advertising and communication in any form and any medium of any work produced or produced within the framework of any artistic, audiovisual and cinematographic project;
