The Origins of
Harmony of Doubt

Before 2017

It all began nearly 40 years ago, even before Jérémy LAVALADE was born.

His mother was not yet 20 and aspired to become a film director. Computers did not exist at the time. She kept a handwritten notebook where she jotted down ideas for films.
Accepted into film school, much to her regret, life did not allow her to pursue the education she had dreamed of so dearly.

In 2017, during a conversation, she shared this notebook with Jérémy. One of her early ideas truly captivated him! He felt an undeniable need to continue what his mother had been unable to accomplish: to make a film based on her idea.

2017 to 2022

After several months of solitary preparatory work, the time had come to test and enrich his efforts by exposing them to other perspectives.

Surrounding himself with other students from ESSEC, including Philippine COINDRE, and students trained in the fields of cinema and audiovisual production, they began to build the story of the film in greater detail: Tana, Altéa, La Pointe Rose...
The deliberately ambiguous title, "Harmony of Doubt," was chosen.

2022 to 2023

The team quickly expanded. In September 2022, work began on writing the script.
At that time, this human adventure brought together nearly fifty passionate, dedicated, and complementary individuals, hailing from business schools, design schools, engineering schools, film schools, fashion, journalism, and more.
On February 27, 2023, the statutes of the "L'Harmonie du Doute" Association, an organization dedicated to the creation of cinematographic projects, were officially registered.

As the project progressed, new skills were added to the team.
The meeting between Jérémy and Pablo FREÏTAS, an actor and director, marked a decisive turning point in the project's development. Along with two screenwriters, Jérémy and Pablo finalized Project 1 by officially registering the script for "Harmony of Doubt" with the Society of Dramatic Authors and Composers (SACD) on September 10, 2023.

Since 2023

To bring this feature film to life, we needed to secure the necessary funding.
In October 2023, the film's team decided to shoot the opening minutes of the movie. By assembling a dedicated crew, Project 2 began: the short film.
The footage created will help better illustrate the island of La Pointe Rose and its inhabitants in the minds of our collaborators.

With this footage, our goal is to secure, by September 2025, the funding needed to produce Project 3, the feature film.
The adventure is just beginning!
