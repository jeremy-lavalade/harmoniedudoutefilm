What are we really
talking about ?

Increasingly facing alarming speeches about the ecological emergency, we want to try something different.
Harmony of Doubt aims to be a communication tool to emphasize the importance of critical thinking on environmental issues. However, we aim to do it with an optimistic and hopeful message for a better future.
We want our audience to feel good and re-motivated, ready to take action in favor of environment, such as the residents of La Pointe Rose (the Pontérosains).

The environmental transition will only be effective if we all act, each at our own level but together.
Every individual is a crucial part of change. If one of us gives up, the entire chain collapses.

We hope that the issues addressed by Harmony of Doubt will become obsolete as soon as possible.
But until then, your commitment is crucial today, whether you are a future viewer, an institution, a business, a local authority, or an association.
