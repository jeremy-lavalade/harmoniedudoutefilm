Their journey

Pablo Freïtas

Artistic creation is a field in which I strive to explore every facet, seeking not only to learn but also to understand this infinite and eternally inspiring universe.

After four years of acting training at EICAR, I discovered for the first time the fascinating world of directing. From my theatrical background, I quickly wanted to steer my work toward cinema. The power of the big screen allows me to push the limits of imagination, through sets as much as camera viewpoints.

Meeting Jérémy Lavalade gave me the ideal opportunity to fully invest myself in this new path. Harmony of Doubt is an ambitious project that allows me not only to collaborate with passionate, committed people from diverse backgrounds, but also to create around shared values of respect and diversity.

I am convinced this approach will help the film stand out, because the tone and the ecological and societal issues addressed in Harmony of Doubt are absolutely essential today. They must be heard by everyone, and I am certain this project will touch minds and awaken consciences.

Pablo Freïtas
