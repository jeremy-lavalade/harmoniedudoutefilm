The film's objectives

To what extent is a society sensitive to the environment willing to surrender its critical thinking to live in a world it defines as ideal?

01

What if it were all just an illusion?

Critical thinking

Critical thinking is the foundation of the film.

In the face of greenwashing strategies and their enticing yet meaningless initiatives, critical thinking enables us to question the real effectiveness of actions, assess the relevance of environmental policies, and promote truly sustainable practices.

As Tana questions the perfection of her island, we must all, regardless of our role in society, maintain a vigilant critical mindset. This is crucial to distinguish actions that are truly beneficial for the environment from those that are meaningless and thus ensure the effectiveness of our environmental transition.

02

Beyond appearances

Environment

Harmony of Doubt addresses constructively the theme of greenwashing and superficial ecological efforts.

By highlighting the gaps between appearances and the actual effectiveness of environmental actions, this film encourages a more sincere and responsible approach to ecology. Rather than criticizing, we aim to promote good initiatives and inspire everyone to explore and support practices that are truly beneficial for the environment.

In doing so, we invite viewers to become informed and proactive actors in the ecological transition by valuing actions that have a real positive impact.

03

Sowing doubt

A thriller movie

Suspense is, for us, a way to captivate and engage our audience in an evolving storyline set on the island of La Pointe Rose.

By presenting an apparently perfect paradise, the film sparks curiosity and sows doubt, encouraging viewers to question appearances.

Through the eyes of Tana, who uncovers the cracks in her ideal world, suspense becomes a vehicle for raising awareness about environmental issues, transforming utopia into a quest for truth that challenges comfortable beliefs.

The utopia will gradually transform into a dystopia.

To defend the core values advocated on the island, Tana will decide — no matter the cost to herself — to expose a system that is not as harmonious as it seems.

Through this search for balance between harmony and doubt, critical thinking and passivity, humans and the environment, we ultimately aim to evoke the joy of boldness and self-questioning — the joy of actively participating, at one's own level, in the sustainable evolution of society…
